# Surface brief: Gratia Sourcing site (all routes)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: overseas home-goods buyer deciding whether to trust a buying house in India. Action: send a sourcing brief. Proof: control of every order shown as signed gates. All facts are placeholder and labelled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A buying house sells control, so the site is a time-and-action order board: rows are live orders, columns never move, only the flaps do, and every order must clear a signed gate before the next. It refuses the category default of factory photo, "trusted partner" headline, stats strip and icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Brushed-steel ground (light, daylight desk), matte-black flap boards with off-white condensed caps, amber lamps for the gate in progress and the one CTA, dim red only for HOLD. Colour beyond that comes from the goods: authored fabric, rattan, brass and glaze swatches in indigo, madder, harmal, jute. Barlow Condensed for flaps and display, Hanken Grotesk for reading. No gradients, no glass, no kickers. Recognisable with content removed: fixed-column ruled rows, split-line cells, eight gate lamps.

STORY: A buyer sees within seconds that orders move through named gates with a named signer, believes Gratia keeps the factory honest, and sends a brief. How-we-work lets them enter their ex-factory date and get a working gate calendar.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Steel header with wordmark in flap cells and a lit "Send a brief" button. Left-aligned display headline "From brief to container, every gate signed." about 6rem condensed black on steel, one-sentence lede, amber primary action plus text link. Full-width black order board under it showing six sample orders (swatch chip, goods, origin hub, gate, due, status) whose flaps cascade in once and advance a row every few seconds, with a pause control and a SAMPLE DATA label.

FORM: Candidate "signals-instruments-split-flap-concourse" (catalog challenger, chosen by the user), fused with the real time-and-action calendar; seed:891f9f4080c5decd. Raise from quilt: category swatches as an unequal patch grid with stitched caption rules.
